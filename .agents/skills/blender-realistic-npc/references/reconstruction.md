# Reconstruction

Select a starting point by evidence, not by generator branding. Single-view reconstruction estimates unseen geometry and often bakes lighting into colour; it is a blockout until the face, side/back silhouette and topology are inspected. Pixal3D output is not evidence of a production rig or animation readiness.

For an identifiable person, keep the original reference immutable. Record crop, camera/view, pose, light direction and which features are genuinely visible. Prefer consistent front, three-quarter and profile views when available. Preserve landmarks and framing when making a working crop; background removal must not erase hair, ears or garment edges. Missing views remain explicitly inferred.

Use local AssetFurnace/Pixal3D single-view only after confirming service/model readiness. BiRefNet can remove a background; it does not reconstruct anatomy. Missing multiview/upscaler/autorig weights mean those paths are unavailable until separately authorized and installed. Do not upload or download as an implicit recovery step.

Use an editable MPFB human when control over anatomy, likeness and deformation matters more than the first generated silhouette. Fit broad proportions first, then sculpt identity; keep a clean editable source and a separate retopologized/baked runtime copy. Generator meshes may be useful sculpting references without being suitable skins.

Gate: matched front/profile/three-quarter captures, stated inferred regions, correct metre scale and editable source. Inspect under neutral light as well as the target scene; inspect untextured geometry to expose a plausible texture on an implausible head. Do not retarget or optimize a face whose likeness is unresolved.

Primary sources: [Pixal3D repository](https://github.com/TencentARC/Pixal3D), [MPFB human modeling](https://static.makehumancommunity.org/mpfb/docs/characters/modeling.html). Check supported inputs and local availability against the installed version.
