# Mercury Clipper reference record

The model is an original reconstruction of **Mercury Clipper, Hunt Mk II, 2017**. Jupiter is used only as the documented sister vessel for hull dimensions and construction details. No Earth/Jupiter name or incompatible later-class deckhouse is included in the asset. Reference photographs are not embedded in its atlas or redistributed here.

## Primary sources checked, 2026-11-09

- [Thames Clippers fleet](https://www.thamesclippers.com/whats-on-board/fleet): Mercury and Jupiter are the 2017 Hunt Mk II pair; Mercury has silver identification.
- [Wight Shipyard delivery release, 22 August 2017](https://www.wightshipyard.com/single-post/2017/08/22/wight-shipyard-delivers-iconic-ferries-for-london): overall length **35.37 m**, waterline length **32.69 m**, moulded beam **8.30 m**, moulded hull depth **2.15 m**, loaded draft **1.03 m**. These are vessel dimensions, not a naval lines plan.
- [Wight Shipyard Jupiter vessel page](https://www.wightshipyard.com/copy-of-35m-catamaran-ferry-thames): independently confirms 35.37 m and 8.30 m, but lists an unqualified depth of 2.80 m. The two depth definitions are not assumed equivalent; neither is used as overall vessel height.
- [Official Mercury photograph](https://www.thamesclippers.com/media/b3gjs0vs/mx102988.jpg?width=2000&quality=90): primary modern black/white livery, aft white `by thames clippers`, singular Mercury Clipper name, front deck, low wheelhouse and continuous side glazing.
- [Builder's Jupiter photograph](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/452e60_52b4bf1eba7a44d6acbb42b7565581f9~mv2_d_5116_3411_s_4_2.jpg): hull rake, independent bows, bridge proportions, swept roof and glazing. Its older MBNA livery is not copied.
- [Official Hunt Mk II floorplan](https://www.thamesclippers.com/media/wm3nr2kw/hunt-mk-ii-floorplan.png), linked by [on-board experience](https://www.thamesclippers.com/private-hire/on-board-experience): saloon aisles, approximate bar/WC masses and sheltered aft outdoor seating. Interior seats are a reduced visual representation, not the certified passenger layout.
