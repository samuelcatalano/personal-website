Total runtime download: **555,524 bytes** (about 543 KiB), three models, 6,342 triangles.
Triangle counts sum indexed triangle primitives in each GLB. The original archive
GLBs reference `Textures/colormap.png`; that source texture is embedded into each
GLB's binary buffer and its image entry now uses a bufferView. Geometry, materials,
UVs, and texture pixels are unchanged; no external textures or decoder extensions
are required. Only selected models ship, rather than the full packs.
The loader preserves their proportions and fits their complete bounds to each plot.
