# Desktop 30 FPS optimization

Scope authorized by owner: reach 30 FPS without reducing graphics quality; measure before and after. No mobile checks, commit or push.

## First slice

The main voxel world's low plants are instanced by species across the entire route. Its global batches submit off-camera geometry whenever any part of the batch intersects the camera. Divide plants into spatial cells, retaining every source record, instance matrix, tint, geometry, material and shadow flag. Three.js frustum culling can then reject whole off-camera cells independently for each camera/pass.

Implementation: `js/world/voxel.js`; update the plant batching assertions in `tests/plantings.browser.py`; add a focused browser regression for transform/color preservation and frustum exclusion. Box batches and grass placement/obstacle logic remain unchanged.

Validation: fixed desktop London route 7–9, clear weather, day, same viewport and drawing-buffer resolution. Capture before and after; compare repeated runs using `tools/perf/route-profile.py`. Run plant browser checks and `node --test tests/`. Keep only measured wins; profile again if the target remains unmet. Record limitations and evidence below.

## Retained change and measurement reliability

Plants are now grouped in 40 × 40 m cells, independently by kind. All geometries/materials remain shared. Bounds are computed before the first render, letting the main camera, shadow camera and reflection cameras apply their own frustum tests. This adds no per-frame plant updates, LOD changes, population reduction or new assets.

The route profiler now boards through the existing intro button, checks that the intro is closed, records the drawing-buffer size/weather, and rejects hidden-tab samples. It accepts `WEATHER=rain` and reports mean FPS. Earlier exploratory samples with the intro's blur overlay open, and one suspended-tab sample, are excluded from the final comparison.

## Final controlled evidence

[Raw samples and summary](../evidence/desktop-30fps/2026-11-21/summary.json). Baseline is commit `bfd2f77`. Chrome uses ANGLE Metal on Apple M3 Max. CSS viewport 1728 × 963, device DPR 2, actual drawing buffer 2592 × 1444, high tier, daytime, London stops 7–9, 12 seconds per run. Same assets, effects, resolution and sampling script in each pair. Two initial rAF samples are excluded by the existing profiler.

| Scenario | Baseline mean FPS | Optimized mean FPS | Baseline median submitted triangles/frame | Optimized median submitted triangles/frame |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Rain, run 1 | 27.02 | 30.25 | 36.80 M | 25.62 M |
| Rain, run 2 | 27.09 | 30.34 | 36.80 M | 25.68 M |
| Clear, one run | 33.89 | 36.40 | 21.17 M | 14.18 M |

Rain improves approximately 12% in mean FPS, with about 30% fewer submitted triangles. Clear improves approximately 7%, with about 33% fewer submitted triangles. Submitted triangles include repeated passes; model topology is unchanged. Draw calls increase slightly because the global plant batches are split into cells.

The rain p95 is 49.9/42.6 ms before and 41.8/41.7 ms after. The optimized rain p99 remains about 75 ms, with isolated frames up to 92–100 ms. This reaches 30 FPS **on average in these London runs**, not a locked 30 FPS throughout the site. The initial conversational 18 FPS observation used a different scene/sample and is not the baseline for these gain percentages.

- `tests/plant-chunks.browser.py`: passed; exact source instance transforms/tints and shadow roles; off-camera clusters are excluded, nearby plants remain.
- `tests/botanical-plants.browser.py`: passed; physical leaf geometry, normalized bounds, materials and shadow reception.
- `tests/plantings.browser.py`: passed through the same-origin fixture (absolute imports; same assertions). Preserves 587 source plants, 16,621 grass tufts, clearances and all 10,186 ordinary boxes. Geometry/material sharing and deterministic matrices/tints checked across repeated builds.
- Before/after fixture rendering with grass, shrubs, flowers, hedges and shadows produced **byte-identical PNGs**, SHA-256 `d5296c2a9a2990fd80d7876cbd82c536da0e26a4bf26f234463571d4d130300d`. This proves that fixture's image is unchanged; it is not an exhaustive visual proof of every route view.
- Final London rain capture inspected. No changes to resolution, antialiasing, post-processing, models, texture quality, shadows, reflections or animation settings.
- `git diff --check` passed.

## Experiments discarded

Runtime batching of opaque parked-car parts, removing redundant post-process MSAA, cache reordering of vehicle geometry and precomputing per-tree wind were investigated. No additional repeatable FPS win justified retaining them; wind precomputation regressed the route. All asset and runtime experiments were reverted. Only plant spatial batching and profiler reliability improvements remain.

## Reproduce

```sh
WEATHER=rain CONFIG=qa=1 THEME=day SEG=7:9 DUR=12 OUT=/tmp/london-rain.json browser-harness < tools/perf/route-profile.py
```

Keep the preview visible. The two runs per version used the original global plant batches and the final cell batches, respectively; retain the baseline commit for future paired checks.

No mobile, night-time, São Paulo/Glasgow performance runs, long-session stability study or full browser suite were performed. No commit or push. GO for retaining the measured optimization; NO-GO for claiming a site-wide, sustained 30 FPS guarantee.
